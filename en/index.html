About

Flowers that outlast a single evening

Listek is a small workshop where every bouquet is built by hand, here. We do not keep finished arrangements in the fridge — we make them when they are ordered.

Flowers arrive twice a week, so what stands in the bucket is fresh. If something is unavailable we say so straight away and offer an alternative, rather than selling you something that will wilt in three days.
